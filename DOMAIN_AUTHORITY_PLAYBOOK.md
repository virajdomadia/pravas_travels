# Domain Authority Playbook for Pravaas Holidays

**Goal:** Build topical authority for "couple luxury travel" in 90 days with zero content creation.
**Why:** 11 package pages + structured data are already SEO-ready. Need external signals to rank them.

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1–2)

### 1.1 — Google Business Profile (GBP) Setup
**Impact:** Shows up in local search, Google Maps, "travel agencies near me" queries
**Time:** 30 min

- [ ] Create/claim GBP at `https://business.google.com/`
  - Business name: "Pravaas Holidays"
  - Category: "Travel Agency"
  - Phone: +919167439172
  - Address: Mumbai, India (from your schema)
  - Website: https://www.pravaasholidays.com
  - Hours: Mon–Sat, 9am–8pm

- [ ] Add 5–10 high-quality images:
  - Hero package images (Bali, Paris, Kerala)
  - Team photo (if available) or office space
  - Trip highlights/testimonial screenshots

- [ ] Fill every field (description, service areas, business hours, photos)
  - Incomplete profiles rank lower than complete ones

- [ ] Get initial reviews: Ask your 3 testimonial people + anyone who's enquired to leave Google reviews
  - Even 3–5 reviews beats 0 for local authority

**Expected ranking:** "Travel agencies in Mumbai", "honeymoon packages near me" within 2 weeks

---

### 1.2 — Review Velocity Sprint
**Impact:** Social proof + CTR boost in SERPs (Google now shows review count in search results for travel agencies)
**Time:** Ongoing

**Week 1 targets:**
- Email your 3 testimonials: "Can you leave a Google review? Takes 2 min, huge help for us launching." (Link: `https://g.page/pravaasholidays/review`)
- Ask in WhatsApp: "Just finished your trip? A Google review helps us reach more couples like you."
- Target: **5 reviews by end of week 1**

**Ongoing (weeks 2–12):**
- After each enquiry → automated email: "Loved planning with us? Leave a review: [link]"
- After each trip completion → WhatsApp message in their group chat with review link
- Target: 1 review every 3–4 days = 25–30 reviews in 90 days

**Why it matters:** 
- Google favors fresh, recent reviews
- Review count appears in SERPs → 25+ reviews > 3 reviews (CTR boost)
- Each review is a backlink to your site (weak, but cumulative)

## Phase 2: Backlink Acquisition (Weeks 2–8)

### 2.1 — Destination Tourism Boards
**Impact:** High-authority backlinks from official travel guides
**Effort:** Low (mostly outreach)

**Target:** Top 10 destination pages (Bali, Paris, Dubai, Kerala, Rajasthan, Japan, Maldives, Andaman, Himachal, Goa)

For each destination:
1. Find the official tourism board website
   - Example: `bali.com`, `parisinfo.com`, `keralatourism.gov.in`, `tajhotels.tajmahal.gov.in`
2. Look for "Travel Agencies" or "Tour Operators" directory/listing page
3. Submit Pravaas Holidays with:
   - Business name + 1-line description
   - Link to your site
   - Phone + email

**Expected:** 3–5 backlinks from DA 40+ tourism sites

### 2.2 — Couple/Honeymoon Travel Communities
**Impact:** Relevant backlinks + referral traffic
**Effort:** Medium (requires engagement)

**Reddit communities to target:**
1. r/Honeymoon (~100k members) — "FAQ: Planning honeymoon to [destination]?" threads
2. r/Couplestravel (~50k) — couple trip recommendations
3. r/Indiantravel (~200k) — domestic trip planning
4. r/BudgetTravel — budget honeymoon threads

**Strategy:** Don't spam. Genuinely help, then mention Pravaas when relevant.

Example Reddit engagement:
- Thread: "Honeymoon to Bali — budget vs. luxury?"
- You: "We specialize in couple-focused Bali trips... [helpful tips] ... Here's what curated packages look like: [link to your Bali package]"
- Result: If upvoted (10+ karma), comment becomes visible → backlink + traffic

**Target:** 5–10 Reddit comments/month with natural Pravaas mentions (weeks 2–12)

**Facebook groups to join:**
- "Honeymoon Planning India"
- "Indian Couples Travel"
- "Budget Travel Tips India"
- "Destination Wedding & Honeymoon Ideas"

Strategy: Answer questions, share tips, link to relevant package when helpful.
Target: 2–3 substantive comments per week

### 2.3 — Influencer Micro-Partnerships
**Impact:** Backlinks + social proof + referral traffic
**Effort:** Medium (but high ROI)

**Don't chase macro influencers.** Target **micro-influencers** (10k–50k followers) in couple/honeymoon travel niches.

**How to find them:**
- Instagram: Search hashtags: #honeymoonplanning #coupletravel #luxurytravel #honeymoonindia
- Look for accounts with 10k–50k followers + high engagement (comments > 100 per post)
- Filter for Indian or India-focused accounts (higher conversion)

**Outreach template (DM or email):**
```
Hi [Name],

Love your honeymoon content! We curate luxury couple trips to [destination] 
— would you be interested in featuring one of our packages? 

We can offer:
- Discounted package for you + partner to experience
- Link from our site (helps your SEO too)
- Feature on our Instagram + newsletter

Let me know if interested. Cheers,
Pravaas Team
```

**Target:** 10–15 micro-influencers over 3 months
- Expected: 50% response rate = 5–7 partnerships
- Each partnership = 1 backlink + 1 Instagram post + referral traffic
- Cost: Free (offer package discount for feature)

### 2.4 — Travel Blogs & Niche Publications
**Impact:** High-authority backlinks from travel media
**Effort:** Low (but selective)

**Tier 1 blogs to pitch (DA 50+):**
- Couple Travel Ideas (coupletravelideas.com)
- Nomadic Lifestyle (nomadic-lifestyle.com)
- Her First Match (herfirstmatch.com) — couples travel

```
Best,
Pravaas
```

**Expected:** 30% acceptance rate on pitches = 2–3 guest post backlinks

## Do NOT Do (Backlink Anti-Patterns)

❌ Buy backlinks (Fiverr, SeoClerks) — Google will penalize
❌ Spam comment sections with links
❌ Submit to 100+ low-quality directories (weak backlinks hurt more than help)
❌ Keyword-stuff your GBP description ("honeymoon packages honeymoon packages...")
❌ Ask for reviews in exchange for discounts (violates Google policy)
