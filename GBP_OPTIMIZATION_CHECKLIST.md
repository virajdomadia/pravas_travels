## Step 3: Add Photos (8–12 images)

**Why photos matter:** Business profiles with 10+ photos get 2x more clicks than those with <5.

### 3.1 — Photo Categories & Recommended Images

**Category 1: Hero/Cover Image (1 image)**
- Use your best package hero image: Bali sunset, Paris Eiffel Tower, or Kerala houseboat
- **Size:** 1200×800px or wider
- **File:** Unsplash image or your best package photo
- **Why:** First image people see — must be stunning

**Category 2: Couple Travel Experiences (3 images)**
- Image 1: Romantic destination (e.g., Paris couple, Bali sunset)
- Image 2: Adventure/Activity (e.g., hiking, snorkeling, cultural experience)
- Image 3: Luxury accommodation (e.g., resort, hotel room, beachfront)
- **Size:** 1200×800px
- **Source:** Use your package gallery images

**Category 3: Team/Office (1–2 images)**
- Image 1: Team photo (if you have one) or office space
- Image 2: Travel expert/founder headshot (builds trust)
- **Why:** Humanizes your business, builds trust
- *If you don't have photos: Skip. Don't use generic stock photos.*

**Category 4: Testimonials/Reviews as Images (2–3 images)**
- Create simple graphics with quotes from your 3 testimonials:
  - "Our Bali honeymoon was everything we dreamed of." — Priya & Arjun
  - "Transparent pricing, zero last-minute surprises." — Deepak & Simran
  - "A dedicated planner responds within 2 hours." — Rahul & Meera
- **Tool:** Use Canva (free) or any simple design tool
- **Size:** 1200×900px (portrait style works well)
- **Why:** Social proof in photos = higher CTR

**Category 5: Destination Showcase (2–3 images)**
- Image 1: International destination (e.g., Paris, Bali, Dubai)
- Image 2: Domestic destination (e.g., Kerala, Rajasthan, Andaman)
- Image 3: Activity/highlight (e.g., couple at sunset, dining, adventure)

**Total: 10–12 images**

## Common GBP Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Using a home address** — Only list if it's a real office
❌ **Wrong category** — "Travel Agency" is correct, not "Tour Operator" (less searchable)
❌ **Fake business hours** — Keep them honest, update if you change them
❌ **Poor photo quality** — Blurry or generic images hurt more than help
❌ **Incomplete description** — Fill every field (incomplete = lower ranking)
❌ **Not replying to reviews** — Reply to every review within 48 hours
❌ **Outdated phone number** — Keep it current and working
❌ **Too many categories** — 3–5 is ideal, 10+ looks spammy
