# Review Request Templates & Automation

**Goal:** Build 25–30 Google reviews in 90 days without being pushy or violating Google policy.

---

## Key Rules (Google Policy Compliant)

✅ **DO:**
- Ask for reviews after good experiences
- Send personalized messages (use customer names)
- Make it easy (give direct link)
- Thank people who leave reviews
- Reply to all reviews within 48 hours

❌ **DON'T:**
- Offer incentives for reviews ("Leave a review, get 10% off")
- Ask ONLY for positive reviews ("Leave us a 5-star review")
- Buy fake reviews
- Ask for reviews before delivering the service
- Use automated review requests (personalize each one)

## Timeline: When to Ask for Reviews

### Phase 1: Initial Seed (Week 1)
**Target:** Your 3 existing testimonial people + 2 recent enquiries
**Goal:** Get 3–5 reviews by end of week 1

**Template 1: Email to Existing Testimonials**

```

Pravaas Holidays
```

**Send via:** Email (formal, one-time)

## Automation Setup (CRM/Email Integration)

### If you have a CRM or email list:

**Step 1: Tag enquiry submitters**
- When someone fills your contact form, tag them: "Enquiry Submitted"
- When they book, tag them: "Booking Confirmed"
- When trip is complete, tag them: "Trip Complete"

**Step 2: Set up email sequences**
- Day 2 after enquiry → send Template 2 (WhatsApp) + Template 3 email
- Day 30 after booking → send follow-up (show itinerary, build excitement)
- Day 7 post-trip → send Template 3 review request

**Step 3: Manual follow-up (if small volume)**
Since you're launching, you probably don't have a CRM yet. Instead:
- Keep a simple spreadsheet of enquiries
- Note date of enquiry + destination
- Day 2: Send WhatsApp review request
- Manually track who leaves reviews (update spreadsheet)

## Monthly Review Strategy (90-Day Breakdown)

### Month 1 (Weeks 1–4)
- **Target:** 5–8 reviews
- **Sources:** Existing testimonials (3) + recent enquiries (2–5)
- **Effort:** Manual outreach via email + WhatsApp
- **Focus:** Seed reputation, establish momentum

**Weekly action:**
- Week 1: Email 3 testimonials + 2 recent enquiries
- Week 2–4: Send review request to each new enquiry (WhatsApp day 2, email day 3)

---

### Month 2 (Weeks 5–8)
- **Target:** 6–8 new reviews (cumulative ~12–15)
- **Sources:** Ongoing enquiries (4–6/week) + trip completers starting to return
- **Effort:** Systematized (template copy-paste)
- **Focus:** Establish pattern, capture trip-returners

**Weekly action:**
- Send review requests to 4–6 weekly enquiries (WhatsApp + email)
- Send post-trip emails to couples returning from trips booked in month 1
- Reply to all new reviews within 48 hours

---

### Month 3 (Weeks 9–12)
- **Target:** 8–10 new reviews (cumulative 20–30)
- **Sources:** Ongoing enquiries + trip returners (from month 1–2 bookings)
- **Effort:** Scaling (WhatsApp group broadcasts)
- **Focus:** Reach critical mass of social proof

**Weekly action:**
- Send review requests to 5–8 weekly enquiries
- Send WhatsApp group reminders (group chat broadcasts)
- Highlight top reviews on Instagram/website (social proof spiral)
- Reply to all reviews

## Red Flags & How to Handle

### Issue: Someone leaves a 1–2 star review

**Your response:**
1. **Don't take it personally** — use it as feedback
2. **Reply publicly within 24 hours** (Template 7)
3. **Offer to make it right** — ask them to reach out
4. **Take offline** — get their contact, discuss privately
5. **Don't ask them to delete** — it looks worse and violates Google policy

**Example:**
```
Hi [Name],

I'm truly sorry your experience didn't meet expectations. We clearly 
missed the mark on [specific issue], and I want to understand what happened.

Could you reach out to me at [phone/email]? I'd love to make this right 
and ensure future trips are exactly what you need.

Thank you for the feedback — it helps us improve.

[Your name]
```

---

### Issue: You're not getting reviews even after requests

**Troubleshoot:**
1. **Is your review link working?** Click it yourself, make sure it takes you to Google review page
2. **Are people even replying to your requests?** Track response rates
3. **Is your timing off?** Try asking post-trip instead of during enquiry phase
4. **Are your templates too long?** People skim WhatsApp — make it 2 sentences max
5. **Are you thanking people?** Social proof builds on itself — publicly thank reviewers

**Fix:**
- Shorten review requests to 1–2 sentences
- Ask in WhatsApp (higher response than email for your audience)
- Ask immediately after a positive experience (don't wait days)

## Pro Tips

1. **Share reviews on Instagram:** Screenshot a 5-star review + photo, post to IG story. Tag customer. They'll likely share it.
2. **Add video reviews:** If someone sends you a photo/video from their trip, ask permission to share on GBP. Video testimonials are gold.
3. **Create a referral loop:** "Know a couple planning a honeymoon? Refer them to Pravaas — [referral link]." Referred couples are more likely to review.
4. **Monitor competitors:** Check how many reviews competitors have. If they have 15 and you have 5, that's your gap.
5. **Use "Insights":** GBP shows you where traffic comes from (Google Search, Maps, Direct). Optimize based on what's working.
